Where time and margin usually leak

  • Growth outpaced the systems. Revenue is up, and so is the chaos. Every decision still comes back to you.
  • You can't trust the inventory number. Stockouts and expedites happen at the same time, and cash sits on the shelf.
  • Late shipments and customer complaints. Orders miss dates and sales and operations are at odds.
  • The business runs on memory, not systems. Key knowledge lives in a few heads. When someone's out, things slip.
  • Overtime has become normal. Tight labor gets worse when process waste soaks up hours.
  • Input costs are squeezing margin. You need cost out of the operation, not just another price increase.
  • A big change is coming: a new facility, a new ERP or WMS, or succession or sale prep. It's a good moment for an outside baseline.
  • One disruption could stop you. Hurricanes and supply interruptions expose single points of failure.

FAQ

What happens on the 5-minute call?

I'll ask a few questions about your operation and what's bothering you. If there's a fit, I'll tell you which step makes sense. If there isn't, I'll say so.

Do you really come on site?

Yes. Site visits are the core of how I work. I'm based in Ave Maria, Florida, and I travel to clients across the US and Canada, on site or virtual. If distance makes more sense, the Founder's Ops Review can be done by structured video with a video walk of your floor. Travel costs are discussed up front.

What's the difference between the Review and the Assessment?

The Review ($2,500) is a half-day second opinion with a short written brief. The Assessment ($8,500–$12,500) is two days on site with interviews, a data scan, an executive readout, and a written report with a 30/60/90-day roadmap.

Who do you need to talk to?

You, plus whoever runs the floor day to day. For the Assessment, that may include your operations, warehouse or plant lead.

How is this different from EOS or lean training?

I diagnose the physical operation: flow, inventory, fulfillment, and where the business depends on you. I'm not installing a leadership operating system or running belt training. Those can complement this work.

How soon do I get results?

For the Assessment, you get a readout before I leave the site and the written report within 5–7 business days, about two weeks start to finish.

What's your operating background?

Before IronRoot I was Director of Operations across four manufacturing and fulfillment sites, and earlier led product operations and inventory control in agriculture supply-chain companies (Nutrien Ag Solutions and Barenbrug USA). Full detail is in the About section.
